Delivery Policy

Last updated 25 September 2026

Quitnafs is a digital service. Nothing physical is shipped, and there are no shipping charges.

What you receive

  • A private plan page, reachable only through your own link.
  • One new step every day for 14 days, each sourced from the Qur'an or an authentic hadith.
  • A daily check-in, a place to log urges through the day, and a progress chart.

When you receive it

Payment confirmedInstantly for Safepay payments, shown on your order confirmation page
Private plan link sentWithin 24 hours of confirmation
Each new daily stepReady by 9am Pakistan time, for 14 days

How it is delivered

Your plan link is sent to the Instagram account or email you gave at the end of the questions. If you gave both, we use email first. It comes from @quitnafs on Instagram or from SUPPORT_EMAIL_HERE.

If it has not arrived

If 24 hours have passed since your payment was confirmed and you have no link, check your Instagram message requests and your email spam folder, then message us with your order code. If we cannot deliver within 48 hours of payment, you get a full refund, as set out in the Refund and Cancellation Policy.
